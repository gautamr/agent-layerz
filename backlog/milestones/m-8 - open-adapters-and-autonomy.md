## Description

An open adapter contract (decision-40, proposed): a versioned neutral model, adapters as pure functions behind a core that applies every safety rule, an open target identifier, per-adapter verified facts, third-party adapters as sandboxed WASM components later. A configurable autonomy level mapped by each adapter (proposal in the autonomy doc). Nothing here starts before the owner approves the decision and the proposal.
